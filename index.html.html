The Festival of Light, Peace, and Enlightenment

වෙසක් පණිවිඩ — Vesak Thoughts

🌸

ප්‍රඥාව (Wisdom)

On this holiest day, we commemorate the birth, enlightenment, and passing away (Parinirvana) of Gautama Buddha. Let his teachings guide your path toward wisdom and eternal peace.

🕯️

ආලෝකය (Inner Light)

Just as these colorful Vesak lanterns light up the night sky, may the profound Dhamma dispel the darkness of ignorance and bring clarity, joy, and peace to your heart.

🍂

කරුණාව (Compassion)

Let us embrace "Amisa-Puja" and more importantly "Patipatti-Puja" by practicing loving-kindness (Metta) and generosity (Dana) to all living beings around the universe.
